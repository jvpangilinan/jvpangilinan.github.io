arcade: doubling-pong.exe

Doubling Pong

A GPU stress test I made in Python and OpenGL, disguised as a paddle game: every ball that reaches a paddle comes back as two. This is the browser version. How many balls can your device handle?

Balls
1
Bounces
0
FPS
–

One ball. Every paddle hit doubles it. Press Start.

It keeps doubling until your frame rate drops below 40, then the balls keep flying but stop multiplying. The desktop version does it on the GPU and reads Windows' GPU counters to know when your graphics card has had enough.
